Explore the 16 personality types

Browse the sixteen four-letter personality patterns. Each guide explains a common style without assuming that a code defines who you are.

INTJ — The Strategic Planner

Independent thinking, long-range planning, and a preference for clear systems.

INTP — The Curious Analyst

Exploring ideas, questioning assumptions, and examining how things work.

ENTJ — The Decisive Organizer

Setting direction, coordinating resources, and moving projects forward.

ENTP — The Inventive Debater

Generating alternatives, challenging conventions, and spotting new possibilities.

INFJ — The Reflective Guide

Looking for meaning, noticing patterns in relationships, and considering long-term impact.

INFP — The Values-Led Idealist

Personal values, imagination, and sensitivity to individual experiences.

ENFJ — The Encouraging Leader

Supporting others, communicating a shared purpose, and building group momentum.

ENFP — The Enthusiastic Explorer

Possibility, creativity, personal connection, and trying new approaches.

ISTJ — The Reliable Organizer

Consistency, preparation, attention to detail, and following through.

ISFJ — The Thoughtful Supporter

Careful observation, practical support, and commitment to people.

ESTJ — The Practical Coordinator

Clear responsibilities, decisive action, and dependable processes.

ESFJ — The Community Builder

Cooperation, hospitality, and keeping people connected.

ISTP — The Hands-On Problem Solver

Practical experimentation, troubleshooting, and calm independence.

ISFP — The Independent Creator

Personal expression, adaptability, and appreciation of the present.

ESTP — The Action-Oriented Improviser

Responding quickly, testing ideas in practice, and engaging with immediate challenges.

ESFP — The Energetic Connector

Spontaneity, shared experiences, and bringing people together.
