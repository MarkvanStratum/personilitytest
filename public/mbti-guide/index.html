What do the four personality letters mean?

Four-letter personality codes combine preferences across four pairs. They are a popular language for self-reflection, but people are more complex than a four-letter label.

Introversion (I) and Extraversion (E)

This pair concerns whether someone tends to direct attention inward toward reflection or outward toward interaction. Neither means being shy or outgoing in every situation.

Sensing (S) and Intuition (N)

This pair describes a tendency to focus on concrete details and direct experience, or on patterns, interpretations, and possibilities. Both are useful.

Thinking (T) and Feeling (F)

This pair is about preferences in evaluating choices: analytical consistency or attention to values and people. It does not measure intelligence or how emotional a person is.

Judging (J) and Perceiving (P)

This pair concerns preferences for structure and decisions versus flexibility and keeping options open. Real behavior depends on context.

Is an MBTI-style result scientifically definitive?

No. Type categories simplify traits that can vary by degree and situation. A short online questionnaire should not be used for clinical decisions, hiring decisions, or predictions of compatibility.
